Overview

Language changes.
What do models understand?

Existing Korean benchmarks mainly emphasize established vocabulary. KoNeoBench brings recent Korean lexical change into focus.

Understanding a neologism can require more than recognizing a familiar string. A model may need to recover shortened source words, interpret a blend, handle an attached particle, or infer meaning from actual use.

KoNeoBench pairs expert-curated lexical annotations with complementary evaluation tasks. It extends neologism evaluation to Korean while examining form, meaning, and context together.

English translations are provided for readability.

A lexical resource with expert grounding

The collection combines automatic candidate extraction with lexicographic review and manual discovery. First occurrences are checked against news records.

Definitions and usage examples support interpretation; formation and category annotations enable distinct evaluation tasks.

Scope

The current release covers the 2020–2024 surveys and news-attested lexical forms. Semantic neologisms—new senses of existing words—are outside this version.

Key findings

What the benchmark reveals

Source recovery, contextual support, and human–model comparisons reveal different aspects of neologism knowledge.

01 / FORMATION

Recovering source words remains difficult.

GPT-5.4 achieves the highest full-set score, but source-word recovery remains challenging across the evaluated models. The 헬스닥 example illustrates how a plausible analysis can miss the actual source words.
